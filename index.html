Account & Data Deletion

Learn how to request deletion of your ClaimIT account and associated personal information.

1. Request Account Deletion

ClaimIT users can request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting the ClaimIT support team.

  1. Click the account deletion email button below.
  2. Use the email address associated with your ClaimIT account.
  3. Keep the subject as "ClaimIT Account Deletion Request".
  4. Include your registered email address or college account identifier.
  5. Do not include your password or OTP.
  6. Our support team may verify account ownership before processing the request.
Request Account Deletion
Important: Account deletion requests are subject to account ownership verification. Never share your password or one-time password with anyone.

2. Data That May Be Deleted

Once an account deletion request has been verified, ClaimIT will delete or arrange deletion of the associated personal data under the developer's control, as applicable.

  • Registered account information, including email address and user identifier.
  • User profile information associated with the account.
  • Lost and Found reports submitted by the user.
  • Images uploaded with the user's reports.
  • Claims and other account-linked records.
  • Notification tokens and associated identifiers, where applicable.

3. Data Retention

Certain information may need to be retained where required by applicable law, to resolve disputes, investigate abuse, or protect service security.

If information is retained, the support team will explain what information is retained, the reason, and the applicable retention period when responding to the request.

4. Request Deletion of Specific Data

Users who want to keep their ClaimIT account but remove specific information, such as a report or uploaded image, can contact support separately.

Describe the specific report, image, or information you want removed. The support team will review the request and confirm what can be deleted.

Request Specific Data Deletion

5. Processing of Requests

ClaimIT will review the request, verify account ownership where necessary, and communicate the outcome through email.

Processing time may depend on verification and whether any information must be retained for legal or security reasons. A fixed processing period is not promised on this page.
